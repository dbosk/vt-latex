\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\Textcite{Knauff2014Latex} provide the only controlled comparison of LaTeX
against a word processor we found; it measures efficiency and errors, not
conceptions, and studies professionals rather than learners.
Variation theory has been applied to computing education
\autocite{ThuneEckerdal2009Variation,ThuneEckerdal2018Lab,
Svensson2020Affordances}; our companion papers
\autocite{VTProgMisconceptions,VTDebug,VTTerminal} extend this line to
misconception catalogues for programming, debugging, and the shell.

Lacking a LaTeX-specific literature, we draw on three adjacent bodies of
work (the systematic search is documented in \cref{app:protocol}).
The comprehension of \emph{compiler and programming error messages} is
well studied: novices find such messages cryptic and a barrier, repeat
errors, and often fail to act on the first one
\autocite{Becker2016CEM,Traver2010CompilerErrors,%
Munson2016CompilerErrors,Denny2021ErrorReadability,Prather2017Interaction};
we transfer these findings to TeX errors in \cref{sec:errors}.
For \emph{markup in education}, empirical research is scarce even for HTML
and CSS \autocite{Kharchenko2025WebDesign}, and authoring structured
documents is a case of \emph{end-user programming}, whose learning
barriers are catalogued by \textcite{Ko2004Barriers}; both inform
\cref{sec:markup}.
None of these studies concerns LaTeX, and to our knowledge no misconception
catalogue exists for document preparation; this paper aims to be the first.

Nor has learners' understanding of LaTeX or of markup been measured
(\cref{sec:protocol-measurement}).
The closest empirical work is the series of studies of novices learning
HTML and CSS by Park and colleagues: a help-forum analysis that finds
students grappling with the formality of the notation---case, white
space, delimiters---and with nesting \autocite{Park2011WebDev}, a lab study
whose error taxonomy shows errors staying latent when the browser renders
regardless \autocite{Park2013ErrorTaxonomy}, and a course-wide log study in
which the errors a validator reported were nearly all fixed while those
in untested code were not \autocite{Park2015SyntaxErrors}.
These measure errors and their resolution, not understanding; the one
instrument that lists LaTeX among its languages assesses the reading of
hierarchies in code \autocite{Park2016Hierarchies}, and the remaining work
measures perceptions \autocite{Loch2015WordMaths} or error logs
\autocite{Kim2019CSSLayout}.
No diagnostic instrument or concept inventory for document preparation
exists---computer science has few validated inventories at all
\autocite{Taylor2014CI}---so the pre/post-test of
\cref{sec:method-instruments} is, to our knowledge, the first instrument
written for learners' conceptions of LaTeX; its design follows the
learning-study tradition \autocite{PangMarton2005,Kullberg2017ZDM} and the
written pre/post measurement of conceptions already used in computing
education \autocite{Pamplona2024VirtualMemory}.
