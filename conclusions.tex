\rqmisconceptions*
So far: the literature is nearly empty---the seed round found only the
efficiency comparison \autocite{Knauff2014Latex}; the catalogue must be
built from adjacent literatures and course data.

\rqaspects*
Preliminary candidate aspects are listed per objective in
\cref{sec:compilation,sec:errors,sec:markup}; the overarching one is the
compile model that displaces the WYSIWYG prior.

\rqpatterns*
Preliminary patterns accompany each aspect; the
identical-look-diverging-behaviour contrast in \cref{sec:markup} is the
paradigm case for semantic markup.

The natural design for validating the aspects is a learning study
\autocite[Ch.~7]{NCOL} per objective, with the pre- and post-test of
\cref{app:quiz} as its instrument: the closed items test the aspects
argued for here, and the open accounts---the first phenomenographic data
on LaTeX that we know of---are where aspects we did not think of will
show themselves.
